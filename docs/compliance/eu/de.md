# Germany

No policy module: resolves to `DefaultEUPolicy`.

## Status

**No country-specific restrictions identified.**

Research found no rule that requires Revel to block attendee invoicing or paid ticketing for
organizers in Germany. This is not a guarantee that organizers have no obligations of their own: as
merchant of record they remain responsible for their tax and invoicing duties.

## What Revel does

- No country-specific restriction: attendee invoicing and paid ticketing are allowed.
- The [common ticket content](index.md#common-ticket-content) applies.

## Legal basis

- The KassenSichV and its technical security device (TSE) requirement cover electronic recording systems with cash functions only. Online shops are out of scope.
- Source: AEAO zu §146a AO (application decree to the German Fiscal Code, §146a).

## Open questions

None recorded. Ticket-specific or event-specific rules (for example local levies) were not part of
this review.

None planned. No issue is open for Germany.

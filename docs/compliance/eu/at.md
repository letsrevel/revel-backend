# Austria

No policy module: resolves to `DefaultEUPolicy`.

## Status

**No country-specific restrictions identified.**

Research found no rule that requires Revel to block attendee invoicing or paid ticketing for
organizers in Austria. This is not a guarantee that organizers have no obligations of their own: as
merchant of record they remain responsible for their tax and invoicing duties.

## What Revel does

- No country-specific restriction: attendee invoicing and paid ticketing are allowed.
- The [common ticket content](index.md#common-ticket-content) applies.

## Legal basis

- The cash-register duty (RKSV, *Registrierkassensicherheitsverordnung*) covers cash sales only. Online card payments are not cash sales, so Revel's online ticket sales are outside it.
- Source: Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF), [bmf.gv.at](https://www.bmf.gv.at): RKSV and the cash-register duty.

this review.
